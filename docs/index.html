01Features

Everything a unit report needs

For defect inspectors, real-estate agencies, property managers and short-stay operators. Built around how you already work: a folder per unit, a subfolder per room, and a lot of photos.

  1. 1.1

    Summary page, filled in for you

    Every defect is listed by room on the Summary page, with inspector, property type and check date filled in.

  2. 1.2

    Photos and videos, in order

    Rooms load from your folders, numbered and sorted. Videos are listed by name in the report.

  3. 1.3

    Type, press Enter, next

    A large preview with zoom. Each caption saves and jumps to the next photo, so a full unit takes minutes.

  4. 1.4

    Check date from your photos

    The inspection date and time are read from the first photo, so nobody types them by hand.

  5. 1.5

    Your company's own layout

    Import any past report, Excel, Word or PDF. Unitly learns its layout and builds every new report the same way.

  6. 1.6

    Six report types

    Defect inspection, move-in, move-out, re-inspection, handover and Airbnb turnover, each with its own title and pages.

  7. 1.7

    Move-out vs move-in, side by side

    Pick the move-in folder and Unitly lines up the before and after photos room by room, with Fixed, Not fixed or New damage.

  8. 1.8

    Severity, who pays and repair cost

    Tag each defect Minor, Major or Critical, choose who is responsible, add a cost. The defect list totals it per party, ready for a deposit deduction or developer claim.

  9. 1.9

    Handover checklist and sign-off

    Keys, access cards, remotes, meter readings and appliances, plus a sign-off page. Sign on screen, or print and sign.

  10. 1.10

    PDF and your branding

    Save straight to PDF to send to clients. Add your logo, company details and brand colour once.

07FAQ

Questions

Something else? Email mohdkaiser1806@gmail.com.

How do I get the app?

After a free trial request or a confirmed purchase, we email your licence key and a private download link. The link works for 7 days. You can request a new one any time with the same email.

Can it match my company's report format?

Yes, with Pro. Import a report your company already made (Excel, Word or PDF). Unitly finds the details, the findings table and the photo pages, and builds every new report in that layout, with your letterhead and logo. Excel and Word work best; PDFs are converted first, so check the layout once.

How long does payment confirmation take?

We check every TNG payment by hand, usually within the same day. You get an order email right away and your key as soon as it is confirmed.

Can I use one licence on two computers?

No. Each key activates one computer. For two computers, the 2-key Pro deal is RM 1,400 instead of RM 1,600. Email us if you need to move a key to a new PC.

What happens when the trial ends?

The app asks for a licence key. Reports you already made are normal Excel files and stay on your computer.

Can I upgrade from Lite to Pro later?

Yes. Buy Pro, then click Upgrade to Pro in the app and enter the new key.

Does it need the internet?

Only to activate, and at least once every 14 days to stay active. Your photos never leave your computer.

What do I need?

A Windows 10 or 11 PC. Reports open in Excel and can be imported into Google Sheets. Saving as PDF uses the Microsoft Excel (or Word) already on your PC.
